5 HR sleep metrics explained in Hinglish for easy understanding

1

Night HR Drop

Raat mein heart rate kitni girri

Avg Sleep HR − Resting Sleep HR

Jab aap so rahe hote ho, aapka heart rate naturally kam ho jaata hai. Yeh metric measure karta hai ki — raat bhar ka average heart rate aur sabse calm state (resting) mein heart rate ka kya difference tha.

Implement karne ki zaroorat kyun hai?

Agar drop 5 bpm se kam hai — user ka body recover nahi kar raha neend mein. App ko alert dena chahiye. Agar 10–20 bpm hai — sleep quality achhi hai. Yeh ek automatic sleep quality signal hai jo bina kisi button ke chalega.
HR metric
2

Recovery %

Neend mein kitna percent recover hua dil

((Avg HR − Resting HR) / Avg HR) × 100

Yeh percentage batata hai ki aapka heart rate raat mein apni peak se kitna percentage neeche aaya. Zyada percentage = zyada deep recovery. Yeh Night HR Drop ka hi percentage version hai — relative comparison ke liye useful hai kyunki alag log alag base HR rakhte hain.

Implement karne ki zaroorat kyun hai?

Ek insaan ka 8 bpm drop kaafi ho sakta hai, doosre ke liye nahi — depends on their base HR. Recovery % ek fair comparison deta hai. App mein "Aapki body ne aaj raat 18% recover kiya" jaise readable insight dene ke liye yeh implement karna zaroori hai.
Recovery metric
3

HR Stability

Neend mein heart kitna stable raha

M75 (upper quartile) − M25 (lower quartile)

Poori raat ka heart rate data leke usse chaar hisson mein baanto. Upper 25% average (M75) aur lower 25% average (M25) ka difference nikaalte hain. Agar yeh difference kam hai — heart rate steady tha. Zyada difference — neend mein disturbance tha, shayad snoring, apnea ya stress ki wajah se.

Implement karne ki zaroorat kyun hai?

Device sensor se already HR data aa raha hai har 6 second mein. Is data ko sort karke M25 aur M75 nikalke automatically detect kar sakte ho ki raat disturbed thi ya nahi — bina user se kuch pooche. Yeh apnea detection ke saath bhi link hota hai.
Stability metric
4

Resting HR Comparison

Aaj ki raat vs pichle hafte ka average

Tonight Resting HR vs 7-day average Resting HR

Sirf ek raat ka data dekhna kaafi nahi hota. Yeh metric batata hai ki aaj raat jo resting HR aaya, woh pichle 7 dinon ke average se kitna upar ya neeche tha. Neeche = achhi recovery. Upar = shayad stress, bimari, ya thakaan.

Implement karne ki zaroorat kyun hai?

7-raat ka rolling average store karna padega app ke database mein. Jaise hi 14-raat ka baseline complete ho (Sheet 2 mein step 1 lika hai), app khud compare karne lagega aur user ko meaningful message dega — "Aaj tumhara dil zyada aaram se tha."
Comparison metric
5

Recovery % Comparison

Aaj ki recovery trend — weekly aur monthly se compare

Tonight Recovery % vs 7-day avg & 30-day avg

Yeh metric Recovery % ko sirf ek raat ke liye nahi, balki weekly aur monthly trend ke saath compare karta hai. Agar aaj ki recovery percentage weekly average se zyada hai — aap improve ho rahe ho. Kam hai — pattern deteriorate ho raha hai, action lena chahiye.

Implement karne ki zaroorat kyun hai?

Weekly aur monthly averages ko backend mein calculate karke store karna padega. App mein ek simple trend graph dikhao — "Pichle hafte tumhari average recovery 12% thi, aaj 18% hai — badhiya!" Yeh long-term health tracking ke liye sabse important metric hai.
Trend metric
